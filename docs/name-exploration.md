# Brand Name Exploration

## Positioning basis

This is a browser-only companion for everyday AI users, developers, climate-conscious people, researchers, and students. It is not a necessary productivity tool. Its job is to make an invisible resource cost visible in a friendly, low-pressure way. The name should be easy to say, easy to remember, and suggest curiosity rather than guilt or scientific certainty.

## Naming territories

The strongest sound territory is **liquid and friendly**. Liquid consonants such as `L` and `R` suggest flow, while nasals such as `M` and `N` add warmth. Fricatives such as `S` and `F` make a name feel light and quick. Plosives such as `B`, `D`, `T`, and `K` add a useful measurement snap when they appear in the middle or at the end.

Literal names such as “AI Water Tracker” are easy to understand but weak as brands. The preferred names create a small story around drops, ripples, sips, flow, and signals without claiming that the extension physically measured a data center.

## Focused shortlist for verification

The first shortlist balances memorability, friendly tone, suggestive relevance, and room for a distinctive visual identity.

### One word

1. **Aquora** — strongest broad brand candidate; water-adjacent but not literal.
2. **Rippla** — most playful and memorable; excellent for a light browser extension.
3. **Flowlet** — best fit for the “small visible flow” story.
4. **Dewly** — warmest and friendliest; lower technical signal.

### Two words

5. **Quiet Drop** — best expression of the unobtrusive product behavior.
6. **Cloud Sip** — most immediately visual and friendly for AI users.
7. **Ripple Count** — clearest link between environmental effect and measurement.
8. **Drop Signal** — strongest technical and research-friendly option.

## Recommendation before domain verification

The strongest brand candidates are **Rippla**, **Aquora**, **Quiet Drop**, and **Cloud Sip**. Rippla is the best fit if the product stays friendly, curious, and visually expressive. Aquora is the best fit if the brand may grow into a broader environmental utility. Quiet Drop is the clearest behavioral promise. Cloud Sip is the most immediately charming for everyday AI users.

Domain availability and trademark status are not assumed from this document. They must be checked before a final name is selected.
